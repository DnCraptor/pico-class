Dr. Mind is a PC adaptation of the Mastermind (TM) board game. The computer
generates a secret color code, then the player has to figure out the exact
pattern through deduction.

Keyboard controls: arrows, return and escape.

Requirements: PC-compatible (8086+), DOS 3+, 100K of avail. RAM, VGA or MCGA.

The game comes in two versions: the normal release and a "LITE" variant. The
LITE version is the same thing, but with pictures removed - may be handy for
computers that have extremely limited storage. Dr. Mind LITE requires only 24K
of disk space, while the full version needs 1.4M (fits on a diskette).
